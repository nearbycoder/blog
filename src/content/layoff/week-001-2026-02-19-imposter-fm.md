This launch adds `imposter.fm` as a dedicated podcast host for talking through this phase of career and engineering change.

## What shipped

- Live site: [imposter.fm](https://imposter.fm)
- Open source repo: [nearbycoder/imposter.fm](https://github.com/nearbycoder/imposter.fm)
- New article: [Launching imposter.fm](/articles/launching-imposter-fm)
- New project page: [imposter.fm](/projects/imposter-fm)

## Why I launched it now

I wanted one place to document what life looks like as an engineer right now:

- integrating AI tools into real product work
- processing layoff uncertainty without losing momentum
- sharing practical perspective in podcast form instead of polished noise

This creates a clear home for those thoughts and makes the work visible week by week.
