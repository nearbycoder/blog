This launch adds `llink.space` as a dedicated link-in-bio home base for my public work.

## What shipped

- Live site: [llink.space](https://llink.space)
- Open source repo: [nearbycoder/llink.space](https://github.com/nearbycoder/llink.space)
- New article: [Launching llink.space](/articles/launching-llink-space)
- New project page: [llink.space](/projects/llink-space)

## Why this mattered

I have wanted to build a personal link hub like this for a while, but I kept putting it off.

Shipping it now solves a practical problem:

- one URL that represents what I am currently focused on
- faster updates as new projects and posts go live
- less friction when sharing my work during this transition period

This gives my work a clearer front door and makes future launches easier to discover.
