This Week 2 launch is [uutil.space](https://uutil.space/).

It is intentionally simple: one place with developer tools baked in so I can use them from anywhere on the web.

![uutil.space screenshot](/images/uutils_space.png)

## Why I shipped this

I keep needing random utility actions while I am already in the browser. Opening separate tools every time adds friction.

uutil.space solves that by giving me one quick destination for those tasks.
