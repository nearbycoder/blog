I launched [llink.space](https://llink.space) as a simple link-in-bio home base for my work.

For a long time, I wanted one clean place where people could quickly find:

- what I am building
- what I am writing
- where to reach me

I just never made time to do it.

Code is open at [github.com/nearbycoder/llink.space](https://github.com/nearbycoder/llink.space).

The app is built with `TanStack Start` and TypeScript.

![llink.space home base screenshot](/images/llink-space.png)

## Why I built it now

I am shipping more in public, and my links were spread across too many places.

That creates friction:

- people miss recent projects
- older links stay visible longer than they should
- there is no single page that reflects what is current

I wanted one URL I could share anywhere and know it represented what I am focused on right now.

## What llink.space is

- A central profile page for my active projects and writing
- A cleaner way to share my online presence from one link
- A lightweight foundation I can keep evolving as I ship more

## What this unlocks next

Now that the base exists, I can iterate fast:

- rotating featured projects
- adding newer content without editing multiple profiles
- testing copy and structure so the page communicates value faster
