EasyAccessQR.com is a focused QR product built around speed, clarity, and measurable outcomes.

## What it is

- A simple QR code generator for fast creation and deployment
- Built-in analytics tracking for performance visibility
- A/B testing support for optimizing destination behavior

## Why this project matters

This project is a practical example of a product pivot done from a stable codebase.

The original foundation came from `dailystand.dev`. Once the core systems were already in place, the move to a different app category was straightforward:

- remove old product-specific logic
- keep the proven infrastructure
- redirect the entire UX around a new customer workflow

## Product and design execution

To avoid generic AI-generated styling patterns, the design flow was reworked using screenshot references from [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/) and then implemented through Codex. That process produced a cleaner and more professional look and feel than a default one-pass AI layout.
