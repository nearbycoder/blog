llink.space is my link-in-bio home base for sharing what I am building and writing.

## What it is

- A focused landing page for my most important links
- A personal hub that is easy to update as priorities shift
- A cleaner way to route people to projects, articles, and profiles

## Why I made it

- I wanted one canonical URL for my public work
- My links had become fragmented across different platforms
- I needed a faster way to keep my "current work" visible

## What I am using it for

- Highlighting recent projects and launches
- Driving traffic to deeper project and article pages
- Maintaining a simple profile presence I fully control
