uutil.space is a simple utility app I built to keep everyday developer helpers in one place.

![uutil.space screenshot](/images/uutils_space.png)

## Why I built it

I constantly needed random utility actions while working in the browser. The tools existed, but access was scattered and slow.

I wanted a single URL I could open fast and use immediately.

## What it is

- A lightweight utility app for developer-focused quick tools
- Built for speed and low friction
- Easy to open from anywhere on the web
